Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to find, buy and follow up on books at Bookstore, including people who use a screen reader, only a keyboard, zoom or voice control.

Conformance status

Our target is WCAG 2.2 level AA. Bookstore is partially conformant: most pages meet the target, the parts listed below do not yet.

Under Presidential Circular No. 2025/10 we also follow the Level A checklist of the Ministry of Family and Social Services.

Middlemarch

George Eliot

About this book

Middlemarch follows the people of a small English town in the years before the Reform Act of 1832. Dorothea Brooke, young and full of plans to do good, makes a marriage she soon regrets. Tertius Lydgate, a doctor with new ideas, arrives to modernise medicine and finds that money and gossip move faster than science. Around them, a banker hides an old secret and a family waits for an inheritance. George Eliot weaves these lives together with patience and humour, and asks how far ordinary people can shape the world they live in.
